# CharityGraph Taxonomy Model

**Status:** Canonical Builder taxonomy guidance

CharityGraph supports multiple versioned taxonomies. A classification is a governed, scoped observation that identifies taxonomy, version, stable term, assignment method, evidence and confidence where meaningful. A subject exists independently of any taxonomy.

Keep regulator source-native schemes distinct from the CharityGraph-native taxonomy. Crosswalks have their own provenance and relationship type; similar labels are not identity. Cause centrality is evidence-backed primary, material, incidental or unknown and is distinct from taxonomy adjacency and downstream mandate rules.

Model outputs, private pressure signals and historical assignments are candidates or maintenance evidence. They cannot create a canonical term or classification without governed decision. Taxonomy changes require a version, migration analysis, validation and release impact review.

The deterministic taxonomy-review workflow remains a bounded private maintenance process. It must not mutate canonical taxonomy files, observations or releases without an approved decision and a separate implementation/release step.
