## Correction

`provider_pre_send_replacements` is an append-only generic linkage. It records the old terminal attempt, new physical/delivery identities, authorization, provider-material hash, and reason. The old attempt is never reopened or rewritten. Replacement creation requires terminal pre-send status, zero crossing evidence, no provider IDs/receipt/usage, an active replacement reservation, and equal provider-material hashes. The stable provider request-item/body identity is preserved; only physical and delivery execution identities change.

Provider-edge certification now checks the exact executor-consumed row, including mandate binding, reservation, attempt, contract/profile, schema/body hashes, route, and output ceiling, before `READY_TO_CROSS_PROVIDER_BOUNDARY`.
