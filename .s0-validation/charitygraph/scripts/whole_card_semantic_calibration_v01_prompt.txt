You are performing a private CharityGraph semantic extraction experiment.

Your task is to identify material candidate observations about the supplied
Australian charity from the FROZEN EVIDENCE PACKET.

This is NOT a writing task and NOT a request to produce a finished charity card.
Produce independently governable candidate observations with precise evidence.

GENERAL RULES

1. Use only the supplied packet. Do not use outside knowledge.

2. Do not fill gaps. If the packet does not support useful observations for a
   section, mark that section insufficient_evidence. This says nothing about
   whether the fact exists in the real world.

3. Lexical presence is not proposition support. A word, heading, URL, program
   name or repeated phrase does not by itself establish a proposition.

4. Keep propositions atomic. Prefer several independently evidenced observations
   to one broad summary that combines distinct claims.

5. Do not create CharityGraph identities. Named programs, services, projects or
   other scopes may be identified using their source-native labels only.

6. Preserve grain. Distinguish:
   - the registered charity/entity,
   - an ACNC reporting group,
   - named programs/services/projects/campaigns,
   - other explicitly bounded scopes.
   Do not attribute a group-scoped fact to the individual charity unless the
   evidence supports that attribution.

7. Preserve temporal meaning. A 2025 filing is evidence about its reporting
   period; a historical event is not automatically current.

8. Every supported or explicit-absence observation requires at least one
   SUPPORTING evidence reference using an exact source_record_id and packet
   locator supplied in the packet.

9. An evidence excerpt, if supplied, must be short and exact. Do not manufacture
   quotations.

10. Do not output a generic confidence score.

SOURCE AND EDITORIAL DISCIPLINE

Treat sources according to what they are authoritative for.

- ACNC register and regulator-supplied structured records are strong evidence
  for the regulatory/registration facts they actually report.

- Statutory/ACNC-filed financial reports are strong evidence for reported
  financial values and reporting scope. Preserve audit/reporting qualifications
  and group scope where present.

- Official websites and narrative annual reports are first-party authored
  evidence. They are valuable for mission, programs, delivery models,
  beneficiaries, commitments and the charity's own claims, but do not silently
  convert promotional or strategic claims into independently demonstrated fact.

- Outcome, impact, ROI, uplift, effectiveness and similar claims must retain
  their epistemic posture. If the corpus contains only a charity's own claim,
  phrase the observation as a reported/claimed outcome. Do not describe it as
  independently established.

- Wikipedia is external editorial/contextual evidence and may establish why a
  fact or event has external notability. It is not automatically authoritative
  for regulatory or financial facts when stronger sources are available.

- PFRA material is authoritative for what the PFRA source itself establishes,
  such as membership.

Prefer the strongest appropriate evidence source for the proposition. Multiple
sources may corroborate the same observation.

NORTH STAR SECTIONS

9. Governance

10. Workforce
    Employees, volunteers and other workforce composition/capacity observations.

11. Capability / capacity / access / availability

12. Relationships / ecosystem
    Partnerships, affiliates, delivery relationships, funders and other

13. Memberships / schemes / registrations / accreditations
    Relevant memberships, accreditation, registration and scheme participation.

14. Ethos / institutional identity
    Source-supported religious, cultural, institutional or other ethos/identity

15. Positions / commitments / implementation
    Public positions, commitments, advocacy, policies and evidence about their
    implementation.

16. Conduct / adverse / compliance
    Material regulatory, court, inquiry, compliance or adverse matters actually

17. Notable context / history
    Material history, milestones and externally notable context.

18. Outcomes / impact / evaluation
    Reported outcomes, impact claims, evaluations and evidence of effectiveness.
    Clearly distinguish self-reported claims from independent evaluation.

19. Classifications / semantic lenses
    Reproduce relevant SOURCE-REPORTED classifications if present.
    Do NOT assign CLASSIE, SDG or CharityGraph semantic classifications in this

20. Evidence / coverage / freshness / corrections
    Material limitations visible in the packet: source freshness, reporting
    scope, representation gaps, conflicting evidence or other evidence caveats.
    Do not speculate about unacquired sources.

MATERIALITY

Aim for broad, decision-useful coverage of the charity, not maximum output
volume.

Do not reproduce every financial line item, every sentence on a website or
every minor event. Prefer observations that would materially help an analyst
understand what the charity is, does, serves, spends, raises, governs, claims,
relates to, and is externally notable for.

Where several observations concern the same named program/service, use the same
source-native scope label consistently.

SECTION ASSESSMENTS

Return exactly one assessment for each section 1 through 20.

Use:
- observations_found when you extracted one or more material observations;
- insufficient_evidence when this packet does not support a useful observation;
- not_applicable only where evidence positively supports inapplicability;
- deferred only where the experiment explicitly defers the analysis.
