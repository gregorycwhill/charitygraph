  frozen demand proposition a deterministic `proposition:<sha256>` identity.

  the packet identity set. The model therefore cannot supply a paraphrased
  proposition label.

  IDs, scope/time state, qualifications, partial support, and fail-closed
  positive conclusions. Seven generic noncanonical-identity patterns are
  rejected.
- `scope_basis` makes direct service coverage/eligibility distinct from program
  metadata, beneficiary categories, address, program location, and operational
  geography. The latter categories cannot mechanically sustain a supported
  service-scope proposition.
- Packets expose the active governed ABN binding as context. It is not an
  instruction that a source must mention the opaque Builder subject ID. The
  Good Friday Appeal request contains its governed ABN binding.
- Nine already-acquired, bound official-site records now have source-native,
  lineage-preserving provider-visible representations. Three remain unavailable
