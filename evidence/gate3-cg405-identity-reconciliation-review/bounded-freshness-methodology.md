# Bounded freshness methodology

The eligible universe remains a source-blind registered-charity legal-entity population. CharityGraph `subject_id` remains opaque. ABN is used only as an ACNC-scoped external identifier and exact join key for exposure exclusion; this review creates no canonical subject and performs no identity promotion.

Five exact legacy/current ABN overlaps are excluded. Each current subject was already a governed record under the ACNC registered-charity bootstrap policy and reuses the frozen ACNC entity source blob; that existing binding is recorded separately from the overlap. Two historical CG405 ACNC-bound identities remain `UNRESOLVED_HISTORICAL_EXPOSURE`: no successor is inferred. Within the declared scope and recorded exposure ledger, the 46 remaining records are `BOUNDED_FRESHNESS_VERIFIED`. This is not a claim of absolute historical non-overlap.
