# Reality Spike 22 — Mega-run 3 synthesis

## Verdict

`MEGARUN3_RAW_TO_PRODUCT_SYNTHESISED` — conditional on a deliberately narrow evidence base. The persisted Mega-run 3 directory contains only Spike 18 / tranche 169. No campaign state or artifacts for tranches 167, 168, 170, 171 or 172 were present at synthesis time. Those missing artifacts are a provenance gap, not zero results.

The available real-subject evidence supports an atomic, attributed G2 proposition spine with explicit satellites and a deterministic projection layer. It does **not** demonstrate Top-100 readiness, broad raw-report extraction performance, or a mature all-section product.

Of 16 comparison pairs, 0 were true conflicts or temporal supersessions and all 16 were prevented from becoming false conflicts. That is a useful safety result, not a conflict-resolution benchmark.

## What the evidence says

The Red Cross revenue pair shows why value equality is insufficient: same period, different accounting definitions. MCMC shows why a current website statement cannot silently overwrite a nine-month FY25 report. The sample retains both source role and scope instead of choosing a winner. These are exactly the conditions under which a card must be a deterministic projection, not a semantic rewrite.

## Counterevidence and uncertainty

* There are no observed genuine conflicts, corrections, or supersessions; routing through those states is only a rule exercise.
* All inputs were already retained; the run supplies no new-acquisition, OCR, raw-document coverage, or provider-performance evidence.
* Five subjects are not a representative Top-100 cohort, and financial evidence dominates the retained examples.
* Candidate activity/beneficiary/volunteering claims have not been promoted; the surface must not imply they are governed facts.

## Recommended canonical tranche

If a canonical turn is authorised, first document—not migrate—the following: atomic proposition projection contract; required display satellites; non-comparable and review rendering; deterministic routing ownership; and a small query/review acceptance harness. Do not treat this experiment as authority for schema migration or Top-100 launch.
