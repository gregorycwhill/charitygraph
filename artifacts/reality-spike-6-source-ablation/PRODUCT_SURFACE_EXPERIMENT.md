# Product-surface experiment

## Fork 1 — regulator-first sparse card vs all 20 visible

**Regulator-first (World Vision V0):** 11 useful sections; the product can lead with identity, purpose, activity, beneficiaries, fundraising, people/scale, finances and funding. **All-20 visible:** the other nine explicit `NOT_PROCESSED` cells make source limits and product promise legible. Choice: lead with populated sections, retain an expandable coverage panel; hiding missingness entirely overstates completeness.

## Fork 5 — absent hidden vs explicit missingness

The Smith Family V0 control has 6/20 positive sections. A compact card is readable but would conceal 14 unsupported areas; the expanded card makes the low-evidence state trustworthy. Show a coverage summary and user-expandable missingness, not 20 blank tiles by default.
