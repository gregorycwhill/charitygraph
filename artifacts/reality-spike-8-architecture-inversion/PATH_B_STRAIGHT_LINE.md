# B — straight-line challenger

`retained experimental observations + their already-declared assignments → one normalized proposition envelope → deterministic section grouping → progressive card and evidence ledger`

The disposable envelope is deliberately one shape: `subject`, `scope`, `proposition_type`, `target_sections`, `value`, evidence/source locators, authority, attribution, time, epistemic status, and computed coverage. It retains structured-regulator versus historical-governed-replay authority. It does not recreate the canonical record family.

Routing is deterministic replay of existing declared `CardEvidence.section_ids`; it performs no text classification. This is important: the experiment proves that the *projection* can be simple once a compatible proposition has an assignment. It does not prove that the upstream assignment, review, or semantic extraction can be deleted.

For all five subjects B has exact section and missingness parity with A. See each `B_envelope.json`, `B_projection.json`, and `B_card.md`.

Retained canonical distinctions: subject and scope, source/evidence locator, attribution/authority, observed/reporting time, proposition value/type, status band, section route, and coverage. Dropped or only carried as an audit stub: typed identity authority, producer/schema/lineage, outcome-state richness, qualifications, supersession graph, conflict resolution, immutable artifact hashes, and formal validation.
