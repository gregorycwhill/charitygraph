No conflicts, corrections, or model-extracted candidates were present in the retained Spike-5 baseline. Their absence is evidence that this experiment cannot certify B/C for those cases, not evidence that the distinctions are unnecessary. Spike 6/7 show report candidates and high-risk relationship/conduct/outcome cases are exactly where the thinner paths become dangerous.
