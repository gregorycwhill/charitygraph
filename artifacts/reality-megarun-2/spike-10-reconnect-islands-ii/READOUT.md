## Hypothesis

The bridge is supported as a reusable *routing* seam across unavailable, narrative, and financial report styles. It is refuted as a universal unlock: it cannot bridge the information gap when the report is absent, deferred, only financial, or uses untyped anticipated outcomes. The bespoke burden is two per-evidence semantic decisions, not an organisation-specific adapter: preserve participant-role lists and refuse endpoint-free distribution figures / future outcome rhetoric.
