# Product decision dossier

| Decision | Campaign evidence | Strongest counterexample | Disposable experiment choice | Consequence | Greg’s decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proposition ledger as product core | 101 five-subject bridge propositions replayed with parity; 12/12 challenger claims preserved provenance/scope/time. | Directed relationships, measures and conduct were not certified. | G2 ledger with evidence, coverage and relation records. | Make claims reusable across cards and queries. | **Yes:** make it the core read model; retain domain contracts. |
| Claim visibility before full promotion | Challenger card exposed candidates with evidence IDs and 16 review units stayed visible. | Candidate status can collapse into “fact” in prose. | Candidate-only progressive card, no canonical promotion. | Faster learning, but status/evidence badge is non-negotiable. | **Yes:** allow clearly labelled candidate visibility, not factual promotion. |
| Progressive card vs 20-section dossier | Seven routed sections and bounded queries were useful without compiler; historical 6–12 sections/subject. | A researcher may need cross-domain completeness and audit detail. | One progressive route per claim plus ledger. | Card becomes orientation; dossier remains drill-down. | **Yes:** progressive card default; dossier on demand. |
| Generic envelope vs domain contracts | 11/16 stress candidates fit unchanged; 5 needed narrow qualifier. | Outcomes, conduct, rich relationships and quantitative measures. | Optional disposable `domain_semantics`. | Avoid mega-schema without flattening meaning. | **Yes:** generic envelope with explicit, versioned domain extensions. |
| Deterministic routing vs model-authored assignment | 16/16 correct capacity/ethos routes; Spike 12 selected deterministic router. | New/ambiguous predicates require human/model candidate suggestions. | Non-authoritative hints; route is a view rule. | Stable cross-section reuse and reviewable change. | **Yes:** deterministic route authoritative; model hints only. |
| Human review amount | G2: 27 review units for 23 propositions + 4 relations; Spike 13: 16 in both paths. | Low-risk regulator-shaped identity facts may be policy-promotable. | Review every candidate/relationship in small fixture. | Review is semantic/risk-dependent, not a compiler tax. | **Yes:** review exceptions/high-risk claims and calibrate automation by domain; do not promise zero review. |
| Batch extraction vs on-demand interpretation | Durable ledger yielded cited 6/6 answers and explicit abstention. | Fresh, narrow question may not justify batch extraction; reports may be absent. | Query simulation over pre-existing ledger. | Query may interpret presentation, but durable claims need durable evidence/candidate state. | **Yes:** batch-extract material reports ahead of broad discovery; on-demand only for bounded, non-promoting answers. |
| Visible sections vs latent dimensions | Section 20 and progressive routes explain trust; 19 is useful query infrastructure. | Forced visible sections make sparse cards read as failure. | Seven progressive routes, not all 20 headings. | Separate user navigation from knowledge indexing. | **Yes:** visible: identity, purpose, work, people/capacity, money, governance, evidence/coverage; latent/on-demand: SDGs, classification, relationships, ethos, history, evaluation, conduct. |

## Decision boundary

These are product and architecture choices. Later engineering hygiene—compiler hardening, adapter cleanup, migrations, tests and provider operations—follows only after the choices above; it is not the campaign conclusion.
