## Quantified change

Historical positive cells: **83/160**. Explicit coverage/missingness cells: **66/160**. Unsurfaced cells: **11/160**. The proposition bridge reproduced the 83 positives and 66 coverage states in the five-subject exact-parity replay; it did not invent a new populated cell. At the campaign level it connected **7 previously disconnected domains to a reviewable route** (7, 9, 12, 15, 17, 19, plus the qualified portion of 18), but only sections 7, 9, 12, 15 and 17 have retained positive demonstration. Sections 16 and 18 remain genuine capability work; 19 is a product surface.

The correct reading is “unlocked representation and visibility,” not “unlocked facts.”
