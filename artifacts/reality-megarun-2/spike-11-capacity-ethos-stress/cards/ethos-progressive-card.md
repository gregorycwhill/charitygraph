# §15 Ethos / values — progressive card

## What the retained reports say

**Australian Red Cross (2024–25 report, pp. 2–4; first-party candidate):** the report makes statements about transparency, accountability and integrity, and records an ACFID Code commitment.

**World Vision Australia (2025 report, pp. 1–5; first-party candidate):** retained material includes a faith-related director statement and corporate/account/audit statements.

## How to read this

These are attributed organisational self-descriptions, commitments or governance-related statements. They are not independent confirmation that the commitments were implemented, nor evidence of beneficiary outcomes or an adverse-risk conclusion.
