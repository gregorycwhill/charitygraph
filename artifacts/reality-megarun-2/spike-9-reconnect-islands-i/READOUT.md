# Spike 9 readout — partial

## Decision

`REALITY_SPIKE_9_RECONNECT_ISLANDS_I_PARTIAL`

The common envelope can carry attributed candidates, route one statement across sections, and surface trustworthy missingness. It cannot unlock the required two rich subjects because the selected retained annual-report representations have no readable native report text in the target pages. The experiment therefore rejects the tempting shortcut of turning report presence, a website summary, or a first-party statement into an independently verified fact.

## Seam classification

Acquisition, report selection, source identity/provenance, PDF representation, governed observations, and card projection are **current**. The source-corpus record also proves report representation is current. The horizontal proposition-envelope-to-target-section adapter is **genuinely absent as a reusable public experiment seam**; it was represented here only as disposable artifact shapes. Rich relationship and evaluation contracts are **reusable historical/current concepts**, not a reason to make a new canonical subsystem.

## A/B

A has the smallest common record and cheapest bridge. It works for a compact participation cue and an attributed relationship cue, but flattens `member`/`volunteer` and risks losing relationship direction/role. B keeps existing-domain richness: participation roles, relationship endpoints/direction/role, and the explicit evaluation-type distinction. It needs about twice the disposable adapter surface (24 vs 12 estimated lines), but prevents the materially dangerous losses. Both preserve exact locator IDs and candidate/abstention state.

## Stress cases

- Governance: no claim extracted. A responsible-person or governance-page label would not prove a complete management/control graph; abstained.
- Relationship: Red Cross’s self-reported Movement membership needs endpoint, `member_of` direction and role; it is not a control relation.
- Participation: the membership/volunteer aggregate is an organisation self-report and is not proof that a currently advertised opportunity produced participation.
- Evaluation: World Vision’s report gap cannot be classified as output, outcome, impact, contribution or causation; abstained.
- Cross-section reuse: Red Cross’s relationship candidate routes to §12 and adjacent §15; participation aggregate to §6 and adjacent §10.
- Tempting claim refused: no “effective” or “impact” claim was inferred from a retained annual report’s existence or from an organisation’s self-description.

## Product consequence

Use a small candidate envelope for the common facts and a disposable mapping into rich relationship/evaluation contracts. On the card, show populated material first with a compact “first-party report / evidence” cue; make the report-text gap discoverable from coverage rather than making four blank tiles. Do not promote or build a canonical section subsystem from this spike.

## Recommended Spike 10

Run a representation recovery comparison on a bounded set of already-retained report pages (native extraction diagnostics → established local OCR/visual escalation only where authorised), then re-run this same A/B fixture on readable report spans. The stopping condition is at least one attributable governance/relationship, participation, and typed evaluation proposition per subject—not prose volume.
