# Reality Spike 12 — proposition granularity, conflict and missingness

`REALITY_SPIKE_12_PROPOSITION_UNIT_DISCOVERED`

This is a disposable, non-canonical experiment. It re-expresses the same retained evidence at three granularities and tests whether a proposition ledger can remain small, attributable and useful when scope, time, duplicates, abstentions and missingness are made explicit. It creates no S0, GitHub, canonical, authorisation, or provider-subsystem state.

## Inputs and boundary

Spike 11 was read first. It records that Spike 9–10 outputs are not present in this checkout; consequently no MSF/Bush Heritage item is selected or reconstructed. The retained evidence used here is: Red Cross 2024–25 annual-report candidates (pp. 2–4), World Vision 2025 annual-report candidates (pp. 1–5), and the retained historical structured-regulator bundles for Sunrise, Noongar Boodja Trust and The Smith Family. All report candidates remain first-party, model-extracted and unpromoted.

The retained material contains no pair of substantively incompatible first-party assertions. Rather than inventing one, the fixtures test the real period/scope near-collisions that a conflict detector must not mislabel as conflicts. That limitation is itself a result: conflict adjudication cannot be validated beyond triage with this corpus.
