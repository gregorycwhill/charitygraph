# A/B readout

## Result

The architecture/product trade-off is concrete. The challenger keeps all 12 retained attributed claims, all scope/time qualifiers, evidence IDs/source roles, explicit coverage for all five required subjects, and four correction/conflict/duplicate review relations. It renders five useful route sections from retained evidence and produces six constrained Terra simulations: four cited answers/qualified non-answers and two explicit no-evidence abstentions (MSF and Bush Heritage).

## Comparison

| Measure | A — historical governed path | B — challenger |
|---|---:|---:|
| Meaningful stages | 10 | 5 |
| Translations/adapters | 8–9 | 2 |
| Record families | 6+ | 4 (evidence reference, proposition, coverage, relation) |
| Claims retained in this corpus | governed replay | 12/12 attributed candidate claims |
| Scope/time and provenance | preserved | 12/12 preserved |
| Useful North Star route sections | historical 6–12 by subject | 7 from retained candidates |
| Missingness | explicit coverage | 5/5 explicit; MSF/Bush Heritage abstain |
| Conflict/correction | governance machinery | 4 reviewable relation units; no invented conflict |
| Human review units | 16 (same evidence decisions) | 16 (same evidence decisions) |
| Disposable glue | ~100 bridge + historical runtime | 154 LOC including fixtures/output tests |
| Runtime | not comparable: replayed artifacts | emitted in `summary.json` |
| Product surface | compiled 20-section card | progressive routed claims + evidence ledger |
| Query-time AI | needs read model | 6/6 bounded ledger answers, cited or abstaining |

The challenger removes projection/interoperability complexity, not evidence governance. It is thinner because it does not translate a claim through typed graph, promotion projection, integrated validation and a 20-section compiler before it can be read. It does **not** turn a candidate into a settled fact: status remains candidate, review relations are visible, and coverage never becomes a negative claim.

## Compiler/runtime deletion test

The prior Spike 8 parity experiment showed that a straight-line envelope retained populated sections and missingness. This replay reaches an auditable progressive card without the historical compiler/runtime. Therefore that compiler/runtime dependency is product-projection migration residue for this proposition-compatible corpus, not product-core complexity. Product-core remains: retained evidence, attribution, scope/time, controlled predicate/route, status/review, coverage, and correction/conflict relations. Rich relationships, quantitative measurement and high-risk review may still require domain extensions; this small corpus does not certify their removal.

## Boundaries

No S0, GitHub, canonical, authorisation, provider-subsystem, source-acquisition, or model-provider mutation occurred. “Terra” is a deterministic constrained-ledger simulation over the retained fixtures, not an uncited generative answer or a paid model call.
