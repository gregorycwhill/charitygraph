1. Red Cross psychosocial-principle claim: qualifier, not satellite — names constrain a program description.
2. Red Cross psychological-first-aid activity: pure G2 plus source/time qualifier; no satellite.
3. Red Cross 70 activations: measurement observation satellite — count, unit and interval inseparable.
4. WVA evaluation practice: G2 plus epistemic qualifier — stated practice, not outcome evidence.
5. WVA 12 / 7 / 5 review count: one measurement observation satellite, not three independent statistics.
6. Smith 5,478 people / 5,983 roles: participation-role satellite; many-to-one caveat is material.
7. Smith volunteer hours: measurement/capacity satellite; do not turn it into impact.
8. ACFID AP4D hosting/funding: directed multi-party relationship satellite; host and funder differ.
9. ACFID policy submissions: G2 plus source-role/causal qualifier; a relationship satellite alone is insufficient.
10. WWF Code adherence: commitment/assertion qualifier; not an independently verified compliance satellite.
