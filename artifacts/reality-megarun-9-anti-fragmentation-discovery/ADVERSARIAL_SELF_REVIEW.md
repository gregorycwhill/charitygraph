# Adversarial self-review

The strongest evidence against the preferred architecture is that even clear report pages require fine-grained epistemic distinctions which a generic triple can hide, and representation selection currently ignores low-quality page text. The direct sample is small and annual-report heavy; it does not validate extraction recall, cross-document temporal resolution, or donor usefulness at scale. The campaign did not acquire a hard image-only/DOCX/XLSX source or a real same-URL byte change, so those remain authorised overflow rather than inferred successes.
