# G2/satellite adversarial boundary

Pure G2 handles an attributed, scoped assertion such as “the report says Emergency Services activated to 70 events.” It fails when the meaning is a bundle, not a single edge.

- AP4D has at least host, funder, initiative and participant-network roles. One generic “partners with” edge destroys direction and funding/hosting distinction.
- Smith Family’s 5,478 persons / 5,983 roles has a stated many-role caveat. A scalar volunteer count loses the relation.
- World Vision’s 12 reviews consists of seven evaluations and five mid-term reviews and should retain method, unit and period.
- WWF’s claimed Code adherence is an organisational self-assertion; assertion/source-role is irreducible to a fact triple.
- ACFID’s policy text says some documents reflected themes without a submission. An influence projection needs an explicit causal-status field, including “not established.”

Conclusion: retain G2 as an attributed proposition spine, plus bounded satellites for role-bearing relationships, participation/roles, measurements, and assertions/assurance. A satellite is not licensed by a label alone: it must preserve fields essential to a real retrieval or product question.
