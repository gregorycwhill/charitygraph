# Direct semantic-wave audits

Wave 1 read disaster/service narrative (Red Cross), evaluation language (World Vision), volunteer participation/testimonial (Smith Family), policy-network text (ACFID), and compliance disclosure (WWF). Ten Terra-authored ledger entries were reread against the shown page text. Eight are directly entailed as carefully qualified reported claims; two are deliberate abstention/boundary items. No item was copied from the run-182 table.

Observed failure patterns: OCR-like mojibake occurs in apostrophes/dashes; nearby metrics can detach from the surrounding program text; first-party claims look deceptively factual; person/role/event measures require structured grouping; policy pages mix activity, commentary, and claimed influence. The audit rate for direct entailment was 8/8 affirmative entries; 0 fabricated findings. That is a small, auditable semantic sample, not a recall or accuracy estimate for the archive.
