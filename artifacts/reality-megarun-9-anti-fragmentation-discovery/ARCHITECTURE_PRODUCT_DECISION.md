# Experimental architecture/product decision

The leading architecture survives with narrower claims: immutable archive binding plus represented pages plus attributed G2 assertions is the baseline. It requires source-role/epistemic qualifiers and bounded satellites for directed relationships, measurements and participation roles. Coverage is independent state. A progressive card is safe only if it exposes its report period, source role, scope, evidence link and missingness; it must route complex questions to evidence ledger/query. No canonical migration follows from this experiment.
