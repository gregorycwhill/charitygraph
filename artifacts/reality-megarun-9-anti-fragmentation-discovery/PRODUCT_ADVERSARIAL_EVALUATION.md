# Product adversarial evaluation

Only run-9 direct entries were used. A progressive card can truthfully say that Red Cross reports disaster-response activity, but must show the report period and link the evidence. It must not imply independent efficacy. A donor asking whether WWF is compliant should receive: “WWF says it adheres; this report also describes a complaint mechanism; independent verification is not present in this evidence.” A question about AP4D’s policy impact must separate submissions, thematic resonance, and causation not established. A query about Smith Family volunteer contribution needs both people and roles, with the multi-role caveat.

The 20-section dossier is useful only after evidence selection: as a default it falsely implies comparable coverage. The card → evidence ledger → dossier/query flow remains preferable, provided every card includes source role, date, scope and an explicit coverage/missingness indicator.
