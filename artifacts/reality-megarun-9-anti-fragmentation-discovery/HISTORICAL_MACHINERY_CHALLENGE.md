# Historical machinery challenge

The observed failures do not justify reviving a page-specific domain parser or 20-section compiler. Current `compact_knowledge.py` already retains scope, temporal values, epistemic status, supporting evidence and qualifications; its absence is that `epistemic_status` is only `supported`/`explicit_absence`, which cannot express organisation self-assertion versus independent corroboration. `direct_service_relationship_projection.py` has a valuable typed-role capability and deliberately avoids inferring roles from prose; that capability is necessary for the AP4D counterexample, though its narrow direct-service vocabulary may not cover policy initiatives. The broader `contracts/knowledge.py` carries source hashes, fragments and observation time. Retain these generic capabilities; do not resurrect rigid document parsing. The run-182 hard-coded generator is counterevidence to compiler-like staging when it obscures provenance.
