# Surface comparison and user tasks

Surface A is the transparent architecture baseline: 20 headings, populated and missing. Surface B is the preferred donor surface: question-led populated content, with expandable coverage. Surface C is the trust/control surface: each compact claim has an epistemic badge and locator. Surface D is a separate AI/search projection: compact context plus uncertainty; it must not be the human card copied verbatim.

## Descriptive task result by surface

|Task|A dossier|B progressive card|C claim ledger|D AI projection|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|What do they do?|Partial: complete map, low scanability|Complete where structured/programme evidence exists|Partial: atomic, reader must compose|Complete for bounded summary|
|Who benefits and where?|Partial|Complete for rich records; sparse controls expose gap|Partial|Complete/partial with source caveat|
|How big is it?|Complete where AIS scale exists|Complete|Partial|Complete|
|Spend / receive?|Complete where AIS finance exists|Complete|Complete but less legible|Complete/partial; must state period|
|Capable of delivering?|Poor: section presence can look like proof|Partial: scale/capacity evidence, no conclusion|Partial: inspect evidence|Partial: explicit non-inference|
|Evidence of outcomes?|Partial in rich P1 records, otherwise poor|Partial; evidence module|Complete as evidence inventory, not outcome conclusion|Partial with uncertainty|
|What to be cautious about?|Complete for processing gaps, poor for risk truth|Complete through coverage panel|Complete: badges/locators|Partial: must preserve caveats|
|Different from similar charity?|Partial, detail overwhelms|Partial/complete where programmes/geography differ|Partial|Complete for indexed distinctions, with attribution|
|What is missing?|Complete|Complete after opening coverage|Complete claim-by-claim|Partial; explicit uncertainty list|
|Can AI use safely?|Poor: headings imply equivalence|Partial|Complete: best source context|Partial: only if provenance payload travels|

The evidence ledger is the safe substrate; B is the strongest ordinary-reader surface. A stays valuable as an audit/debug view, not a default. D is useful only if it carries claim-level source and epistemic fields alongside text.
