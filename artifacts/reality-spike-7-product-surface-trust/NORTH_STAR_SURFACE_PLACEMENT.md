# North Star surface placement

North Star remains the completeness and anti-drift contract; this is a product placement decision, not a contract amendment.

|Placement|Sections|Reason|
|---|---|---|
|Core default|1 identity, 2 purpose, 3 programmes, 5 beneficiaries/geography, 11 scale, 13 finances|Answer the first donor questions when evidence exists.| 
|Expandable detail|4 activities/SDGs, 8 fundraising, 10 people, 14 funding, 17 history|Useful context but not a default reading burden.| 
|Evidence/coverage module|6 participation, 7 capacity, 9 governance, 12 relationships, 15 ethos, 16 conduct, 18 outcomes, 20 evidence|High semantic sensitivity or strong dependence on evidence context; show when material and link evidence.| 
|Search/AI-only capability|19 classification/search/AI discovery|Taxonomy is useful for retrieval and matching, but a permanent reader-facing tile is dead or confusing surface in this cohort.| 
|Possibly not directly user-facing|Internal freshness/correction mechanics within 20|Expose a concise coverage result; retain full mechanics for evidence detail and operators.| 

Default does not mean “hide missingness.” The card should say, for example, “11 of 20 North Star areas have retained evidence; 9 are not processed” and offer the exact coverage panel.
