P0 shows structured regulator/source facts (and any already promoted history) only. P1 additionally shows bounded report candidates with the inline badge `MODEL-EXTRACTED FROM REPORT — FIRST-PARTY CLAIM, NOT INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED` and a source/page locator. ## Interpretation

P0 is safe but loses the material difference between a report-rich international service organisation and a thin regulator record. P1 unlocks 16 report-derived groups without saying they are independent facts; its review queue remains visible and bounded. P2 unlocks no additional content over P1, merely removes the reader’s warning. It therefore adds misleadingness and editorial risk without product-value gain.

P1 should retain visual separation from regulator facts, a first-party qualifier, report year/page locator, and a plain-language warning. It is acceptable only for descriptive, non-evaluative uses pending claim-type gating described in `NEUTRALITY_STRESS.md`.
