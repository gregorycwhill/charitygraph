# Reality Spike 7 — final readout

`REALITY_SPIKE_7_PRODUCT_TRUST_DISCOVERED`

1. **Strongest surface pattern:** a progressive donor card (B), with evidence ledger (C) as the truth surface, audit dossier (A) behind it, and a separate constrained AI projection (D).
2. **Trust result:** P1 provides the material content gain—16 report candidate groups across World Vision and Red Cross—while keeping their first-party/model status obvious. P0 is thin; P2 adds no content over P1 and erases the key warning.
3. **Review scaling:** 16 candidates cost roughly 8–24 minutes at 30–90 sec each. Mechanical extrapolation ranges from ~267–800 hours at 10,000 using the sparse sample mix, or ~667–2,000 hours at the report-rich rate. These are scenario bands, not forecasts.
4. **North Star placement:** identity/purpose/programmes/beneficiaries/scale/finances are default when present; sensitive/semantic sections are evidence-led detail; classification is latent/search capability. North Star itself remains unchanged.
5. **Hard governance exceptions:** ROI/uplift, outcome conclusions, conduct/adverse matters, material governance quality claims, and relationship assertions require higher gates than attribution.
6. **User tasks:** B is strongest for ordinary orientation; C is strongest for provenance and safe AI context; A is strongest for coverage; D is useful only with retained uncertainty/provenance.
7. **Forks:** choose P1 for low-risk descriptive visibility, progressive disclosure, inline provenance, coverage panel, separated fact bands, separate AI output, and latent classification.
8. **Decisions exposed now:** build the claim/evidence model and P1 gate; avoid a 20-tile default and P2; define high-risk claim routing; preserve missingness and source context in AI payloads.
9. **Next tranche:** architecture simplification. The uncertainty is not Top-100 acquisition feasibility; it is whether a current retained-representation → candidate/claim/evidence renderer can make P1 safe, repeatable, and reviewable without reusing experimental glue.
