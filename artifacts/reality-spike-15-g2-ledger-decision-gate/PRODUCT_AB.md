# Product A/B

## Result

Default to a progressive card with clearly labelled unpromoted first-party claims, evidence/period chips and a coverage drawer. Keep a 20-section dossier on demand; it is unsuitable as the default surface for partial evidence. Batch-precompute material reports into the ledger; allow lazy Q&A only to format/retrieve existing claims or return an abstention.

| Subject | Baseline/current historical card | G2 progressive card | Ledger/dossier value | Q&A result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Australian Red Cross | report presence plus low-text gap; official-site facts isolated | participation, movement relation, service/capacity, governance, ethos and evaluation-boundary cues | 7 claims, 1 coverage item, source-level drill-down | six-domain questions answered with citations or current-availability abstention |
| World Vision | report presence plus low-text gap | governance, relationship, audit, speaker-scoped ethos and evaluation-material cue | 5 claims, 1 coverage item; no false impact conclusion | governance/relationship/ethos answered; outcomes question abstains |
| Bush Heritage | impact-report page isolated | participation and named co-design relationship | 2 claims, 1 coverage item; rich relationship detail visible | participation/relationship answered; evaluation abstains |

### Constrained ledger Q&A

| Question | Answer behaviour |
|---|---|
| “Does Red Cross have a current volunteer opportunity?” | Abstain: retained evidence is an undated aggregate and 2024–25 report statement, not current availability. |
| “Who is on World Vision’s board?” | Return the time-bound report statement and locator, not a claimed current complete roster. |
| “What relationship does Bush Heritage report?” | `co_designed_with` Dja Dja Wurrung Clans Aboriginal Corporation; role and endorsement preserved. |
| “Has World Vision demonstrated impact?” | Abstain: material existence is not a typed finding. |
| “What ethos claims are reported?” | Return attributed ARC commitment/value and WVA speaker-scoped faith statement with status badge. |

Candidate visibility is product-useful but only with a non-factual label, source role, period/scope and a ledger link. The 20-section view stays available for researchers who need coverage and evidence detail.
