# Top-100 update (bounded estimate)

Fixture rate: 14 propositions / 3 evidence-bearing rich subjects = 4.7 propositions per rich subject; 4 abstentions and 4 coverage records across four subjects. Review units are 18: 14 candidates + 4 scope/time/relation decisions (1.3 per proposition). A conservative Top-100 plan should expect 300–500 material candidate propositions, 100 coverage records, and 390–650 review units before calibrated low-risk automation—not 100 fully comparable dossiers.

Batch precompute is preferred for material annual reports because it preserves page/source lineage, makes coverage visible and supports repeatable cards/Q&A. Lazy report semantics can answer a bounded question without promotion, but must not silently create durable facts or hide unprocessed coverage.

Biggest obstacle to Top-100 product value: reliable readable, attributable report representation and review triage—not the generic ledger schema. High latent report value cannot become product value while low-text/OCR/vision recovery is partial and while rich-domain evidence needs qualified review.
