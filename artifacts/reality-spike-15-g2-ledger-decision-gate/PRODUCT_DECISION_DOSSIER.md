# Product decision dossier

## Recommended decision (not authorised by this spike)

Adopt G2 as the durable attributed-proposition spine with separate coverage/missingness and relation records. Do **not** treat it as a universal semantic replacement: participation opportunity lifecycle, governance graphs, relationship graphs, operational service/capacity, and especially evaluation/measurement records remain first-class attached domain structures. The evidence supports a spine-plus-satellites architecture.

## Evidence, unknowns and strongest counterevidence

The core evidence is real retained first-party report/official-page material with exact historical IDs and locators, not report-presence inference. ARC contributes participation, relationship, service/capacity, audit/control and ethos candidates; WVA contributes governance, relationship, audit, speaker-scoped ethos and evaluation-boundary candidates; BHA supplies native-text participation and co-design relationship evidence. MSF demonstrates faithful absence. Scope/time/source distinctions prevent four false conflicts; coverage remains separate.

The strongest counterevidence is that original retained report pages are still mostly low-native-text (ARC pp.6–122; WVA pp.6–51), so the spike relies on limited historically recovered ranges. It also found no verified substantive conflict, and the evaluation/outcomes section improves chiefly by correct evidence-boundary statements, not a real outcome result. G2 would be too thin if satellites were discarded: endpoint/direction/role, measurement denominator, assertion stage, and evaluation method/result cannot be flattened safely.

Unknowns: recovery yield and review rates across a representative Top-100 cohort; whether a mature relation/evaluation satellite can share IDs cleanly with G2; calibration for candidate visibility; and a real correction/conflict pair. The next product decision is whether to approve G2-plus-satellites and the progressive evidence-ledger surface. Later engineering hygiene—schema, compiler cleanup, migrations, extraction tuning and tests—is deliberately separate.
