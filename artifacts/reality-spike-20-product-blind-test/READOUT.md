# Reality Spike 20 — progressive product blind test

Evaluated **10 cards** and **40 questions**: 20 useful grounded answers and 20 explicit abstentions.

## Verdict

The proposition ledger is a better product spine than a dossier-first or AIS-only surface: it keeps each answer reviewable. It is not sufficient alone for interpretation. The progressive card should be default; a dossier is required for statement context and broad coverage inspection.

## Evidence limits and trust

- A related-party Yes/No must not appear as a propriety judgement.
- A purpose description must not appear as measured impact.
- A visible financial line without statement title/comparative context invites misleading ratios.
- Coverage-only cards must lead with unavailable representation, not a blank optimistic card.

## Product changes implied

- Make candidate/reported status and reporting period persistent on every visible answer.
- Put coverage gaps beside each card section, with an abstain response as a first-class result.
- Use progressive card as default; open the dossier for financial context, conflicts, and breadth inspection.
- Keep provenance IDs and locators copyable at answer level; do not require a user to infer their source.
