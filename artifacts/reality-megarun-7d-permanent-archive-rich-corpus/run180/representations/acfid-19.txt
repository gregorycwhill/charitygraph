Sephrose, a mother of four, lives in Cankuzo. For over a year, she has
been part of a farming group that belongs to the LWF project. The
project has provided Sephrose knowledge on what to plant, where
to get the seeds, and how to irrigate, helping her to have a good
harvest. She says: “Now my children are better nourished thanks to
the bigger and better crops that I got”. Photo: LWF Burundi
