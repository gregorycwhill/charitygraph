Cover photo: Nature-based
solutions harness the power of
nature to boost natural ecosystems,
biodiversity and human well-being
to address major societal issues,
including climate change.
In Solomon Islands, drawing on
traditional practices is helping to
conserve sea grapes, a critical food
source in the face of a changing
climate to ensure they can be
enjoyed for generations to come.
This is part of the project, Nature-
Positive Business for Climate Critical
Ecosystems, supported by Climate
Resilient by Nature (CRxN), an
Australian Government initiative in
partnership with WWF-Australia.
The project aims to maintain and
enhance the functionality of climate
critical ecosystems including
reefs and mangroves through
community-led businesses.
Pictured: Nerolyn, a community
facilitator, harvesting sea grapes.
