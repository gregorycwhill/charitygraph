Many hands truly do make light work. In 2025-26, our
volunteers contributed more than 47,235 hours. This
generous support will ultimately help us double and
deepen our impact by 2030.
Our volunteers were able to build connections with
a diverse cohort across the country, whether it was
through helping our agricultural project at Victoria’s
Turnip Creek or by working behind the scenes from
home.
