Building Climate
Resilience for the future
The effects of climate change are becoming increasingly severe, and it is the
world’s poorest communities—those least responsible for global warming—that
are suffering the most. These communities often live in the most vulnerable areas
and lack the resources to adapt to the growing frequency of climate-related
disasters. At Plan International we are committed to supporting communities to
build climate resilience, especially children, women, and marginalized groups.
