A certified path to restoration
In south-west Western Australia, biodiversity
certification and carbon sequestration are delivering
strong outcomes for nature while unlocking new
revenue pathways.
Ediegarrup Reserve is located 400 kilometres south-
east of Perth, on the traditional lands of the Goreng
Noongar people. On its way to becoming a thriving
refuge, we’ve worked with Greening Australia, Nowanup
Caretakers (Rangers) and Badgebup Aboriginal
Corporation's Ngoolark Rangers to plant 480 kilograms
of seed and 154,000 seedlings over three years. This
represents 135 local native species across over 550
hectares of Ediegarrup’s previously cleared farmland.
This restoration began after we completed baseline
monitoring on-site in 2023.
