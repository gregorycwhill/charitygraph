Discussion and Analysis –
Consolidated Statement of
Financial Position
Changes in the composition of assets
and liabilities
Bush Heritage’s Total Equity at 31 March 2026 of
$95.5 million was a slight decrease of $1.0 million from
the prior year. Total Equity represents the accumulated
supporter contributions and other net earnings that
Bush Heritage and its controlled entity have received
since their inception, and which have been applied
to the acquisition of land and other capital assets for
conservation purposes.
Property, plant and equipment increased by $5.9 million
in 2025-26, which included the acquisition of the
Avocet property in Queensland, Western Kangoulu
Country, and the recognition of a $3.2 million Carbon
Sink Asset. This new asset recognition represented
the accumulated restoration and establishment costs
incurred by third party contribution at the Ediegarrup
Reserve in Western Australia under Bush Heritage’s
carbon sequestration project. A corresponding non-
current liability of $3.2 million was recognised reflecting
Bush Heritage’s obligation to the third party. The liability
will be progressively settled through the transfer of
Australian Carbon Credit Units (ACCUs) as they are
issued by the Clean Energy Regulator over the 25-year
life of the project.
At 31 March 2026 Bush Heritage had cash and
cash equivalents of $3.6 million and $32.1 million
in investments.
Debt and equity
