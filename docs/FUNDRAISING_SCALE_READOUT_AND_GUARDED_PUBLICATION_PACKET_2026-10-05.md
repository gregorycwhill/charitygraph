## Safe conclusion

CharityGraph can now retain and query *reviewed, subject-bound, source-scoped*
observations of fundraising practice as multiple facets rather than a single
method label.  It can distinguish a standing practice from a campaign, a
provider relationship and a funding-source/finance fact; retain source-native
wording, locator, time, scope, coverage and review state; and expose an
explicitly linked finance fact without inventing attribution.

Examples of safe outputs are: a first-party statement supporting a direct
contact practice facet; a dated F2F assertion whose lifecycle is `current`,
`recent`, `historical`, `discontinued`, `unknown` or `not_found_in_scoped_source`;
an explicitly named campaign; and a disclosed organisation-level fundraising
expense beside a method observation with the result `unattributed`.  PFRA
membership, regulator registration, generic donation copy, and scoped silence
remain non-inferences.

It cannot yet claim prevalence, organisation-wide continuity, a universal F2F
currentness rate, channel performance, or that aggregate income/cost was
generated by a method, campaign or provider.  It cannot publish ROI,
cost-to-raise-a-dollar, profitability, donor-acquisition or causal claims.

## Finance and multiyear disposition

Direct disclosure is preferred, then deterministic reconstruction, then
governed bounds/interpretation; otherwise values stay unavailable.  Measurement
compatibility gates entity, period, scope, accounting basis, gross/net and flow
meaning before comparison.  Multiyear representation is supported only when
those dimensions remain explicit and compatible.  The bounded cohort proves
the gates and representations, not coverage of real multi-year disclosures.

No aggregate expense/income allocation occurred.  A practice observation next
to organisation-level finance is deliberately non-attributed unless an
explicit lower-grain money link exists; campaign and provider links are equally
non-causal.  The 0/14 share-or-bounds result is a release blocker for any
method-share statement, not an invitation to estimate one.
