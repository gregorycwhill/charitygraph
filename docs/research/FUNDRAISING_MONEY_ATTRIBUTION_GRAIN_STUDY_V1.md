# Fundraising money-attribution grain study v1

**Status:** research-only, reproducible evidence study; not production ingestion.

As at 2026-10-04, this retained/local-public cohort has **16** cases. Target was 20-30; shortfall: Only 16 cases met the inclusion rule without downloading a new corpus: each preserves a public/local locator and either a source-native money metric or a directly relevant negative/coverage case. Regulator guidance is included as evidence about available grain, not as a charity amount.

## Finding

The evidence supports money observations most often at organisation or fundraising-portfolio grain. Appeal- and provider-relationship grain are strongly supported as record-keeping/reporting possibilities by NSW rules, but no retained public charity return supplies a reusable amount. Campaign-platform/benchmark sources can support campaign-reported amounts only when the named row, metric definition and subject binding are retained. No aggregate total may be allocated downward merely because a practice/campaign/provider is also observed.

| Smallest justified scope | Cases | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| organisation | 3 | common in retained financial statements |
| fundraising_portfolio | 2 | common enough for direct/functional total only |
| standing_practice_or_channel | 1 | rare for money; PFRA supports practice evidence, not amount |
| campaign | 3 | possible but unsupported in frozen cohort without a retained benchmark row |
| appeal | 4 | regulator-required/possible, no retained public amount |
| provider_relationship | 3 | possible operational record, no retained attributed amount |

## Concrete negative cases

- **c14:** a source-native mixed `Donations, Fundraisings, Lectures` row is organisation-level only; splitting it changes the source claim.
- **c15:** a direct fundraising functional allocation (and its mechanical approximate amount) is a portfolio total; observed channels/campaigns do not establish components or additivity.
- **c04/c07:** a trader rule or provider directory does not evidence a charity-provider contract, much less a share of income or expenditure.
- **c16:** even explicit expense labels can support a bounded portfolio construct in a test fixture without identifying channels; it is deliberately non-publishable.
