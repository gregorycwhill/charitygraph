## What is now achieved

CharityGraph has a governed local capability to retain reviewed,
subject-bound, source-scoped fundraising observations without flattening
fundraising into one method.  It distinguishes standing practice, campaign,
provider relationship and money/finance fact; preserves native wording or a
locator, source role, binding basis, observed/valid time, scope, production
method, review status and coverage; and fails closed where evidence is absent.

Specific-method and F2F observations can carry a source-bounded lifecycle:
`current`, `recent`, `historical`, `discontinued`, `unknown`, or
`not_found_in_scoped_source`.  PFRA membership alone is not practice or
currentness evidence.  Campaign/provider identity, setting, agency,
compensation, regular giving, organisation-wide scope and continuity remain
unknown unless directly supported.

Finance can retain direct source-native disclosure and deterministic
reconstruction only when entity, period, basis, metric/flow meaning,
gross/net treatment, components and smallest grain are compatible.  Aggregate
money stays explicitly `unattributed` beside a method/campaign/provider unless
the source supplies an exact-grain link.  No share, net proceeds, ROI,
cost-to-raise, effectiveness or causal result is emitted.
