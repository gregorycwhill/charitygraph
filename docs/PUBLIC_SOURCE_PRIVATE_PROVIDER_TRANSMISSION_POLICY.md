# Public-source private-provider transmission policy

**Authority:** Product Owner decision, 17 October 2026.

For governed, lawfully acquired public PDF and XLSX source material, CharityGraph may transmit a complete source-native document or workbook to the configured model provider for private analytical processing. This is an affirmative transmission permission under `CG_PUBLIC_SOURCE_PRIVATE_PROVIDER_TRANSMISSION_V1`.

This policy does not permit non-public material, public redistribution, missing provenance, unbound subjects, bypassed access controls, provider data sharing/training opt-in, or any Source Universe v1 expansion. Exact source, acquisition lineage, rendered representation and provider-policy bindings remain mandatory.

## Rights and payload planning are separate

Rights answers whether the full governed public source may be transmitted. It has no page, percentage, sheet, range or cell cap. Payload planning may choose a bounded excerpt, sheet/range, structured representation or complete source-native rendering for cost, latency and context efficiency. That choice must retain source and representation lineage and must never be read as a conclusion that the full public source was prohibited.

The historical `AU_FAIR_DEALING_ANALYTICAL_PROCESSING_V1` record is retained unchanged for its historical bounded-processing decisions. It is not the governing transmission policy for the above public PDF/XLSX scope. `PUBLIC_FACTS_ONLY_V1` remains available where a structured factual representation is independently selected; it is not required merely because the source is a public PDF/XLSX.
